# Turtle WoW 1.17.2 General Changes - Formulas Affecting Damage/Healing

## Resource Generation Formula
- **New Formula Approach**: 90% gear-dependent + 10% weapon speed predetermined
- **Design Principle**: "The top is the same, but the bottom has been raised up"

## Scaling Improvements
- Attack Power scaling added to many abilities that previously had flat damage
- Spell Power scaling added to many abilities that previously had flat damage
- Percentage-based scaling replacing flat values
- Better scaling for both early and late game
